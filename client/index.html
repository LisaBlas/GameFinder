Game Finder: find your next video game by what you actually enjoy

GameFinder is a free game finder website and video game recommendation tool. Discover games to play using thousands of specific keywords: combine gameplay mechanics, settings, aesthetics and moods (cozy, roguelike, dark fantasy, city builder) to find games by mood and playstyle. Add genre, platform and game mode, start from a game you already love, or explore the keyword map to reach games a normal store search would not show.

Every result links to official stores, with gameplay previews and a short reason why the game matches your search.

Game finder questions

What is GameFinder?

GameFinder is a free game finder. Instead of searching by title or genre alone, you pick curated keywords for mood, mechanics, setting and style, and it shows games that match them.

How is GameFinder different from other game finders?

It starts from what you want to feel or do, such as a cozy farming loop or a dark fantasy souls-like. A keyword map shows which ideas combine well, so you can reach games a store search would not show.

Is GameFinder free?

Yes. GameFinder is free and needs no account.

Where do the games come from?

Game data comes from IGDB. Each game links to official stores and, where available, partner marketplaces.
